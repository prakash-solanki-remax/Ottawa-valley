Choosing a Real Estate Agent in Pembroke & Petawawa: Key Questions to Ask Before You Start

Published by Prakash Solanki, REALTOR® | RE/MAX Pembroke Realty Ltd., Brokerage

Whether you are planning to buy a home or sell a property in Pembroke, Petawawa, or nearby Ottawa Valley communities, choosing the right real estate representation is an important early decision. A strong working relationship starts with clear communication, honest expectations, and a solid grasp of local neighbourhoods and Ontario real estate rules.

1. How Well Do You Know Pembroke, Petawawa & Surrounding Areas?

Local market conditions can shift from street to street and township to township. Asking how an agent tracks local inventory, neighbourhood dynamics, and property considerations in Pembroke, Petawawa, Laurentian Valley, and surrounding areas helps you see whether their day-to-day focus matches your goals.

2. How Does Client Representation Work Under Ontario Rules?

In Ontario, real estate transactions are governed by RECO and TRESA rules, which set clear standards around disclosure, written representation agreements, and how client interests are protected. A good agent will explain how representation works in plain language before you sign anything so you feel informed at every stage.

3. What Is Your Communication & Next-Steps Process?

Buying or selling involves many moving parts, from reviewing property details and scheduling showings to coordinating offers, inspections, and closing timelines. Ask how often you will receive updates, how quickly questions are answered, and what the first practical step looks like for your timeline.

  • For Buyers: Clarify financing readiness, must-have features, and how you will evaluate comparable properties before making an offer.
  • For Sellers: Discuss realistic pricing strategy, preparation priorities, and how your home will be presented and marketed.
  • Brokerage Support: Working through an established local brokerage like RE/MAX Pembroke Realty Ltd., Brokerage provides office resources, compliance oversight, and local market tools.
