As home to 4th Canadian Division Support Base (4 CDSB) Garrison Petawawa, the Ottawa Valley experiences a distinct annual housing cycle tied directly to Canadian Armed Forces (CAF) posting directives. While formal posting messages are typically issued in the spring ahead of summer relocations, early planning behind the scenes starts long before your official House Hunting Trip (HHT) window opens.

Whether you are anticipating a posting instruction into Garrison Petawawa or preparing to list your current home for an outbound move across Ontario, establishing a clear real estate plan early ensures a smooth, stress-free transition for your family.

Understanding the Posting & SIRVA Timeline

With SIRVA operating as the Contracted Relocation Service Provider (CRSP) under the Canadian Armed Forces Relocation Directive (CAFRD), setting up your portal early is key. Early market research in late fall and winter allows military members to define budget parameters, explore neighborhood micro-markets, and monitor local pricing trends before active House Hunting Trips begin.

Essential Planning Checkpoints for Relocating Families

1. Mortgage Pre-Approval & Budgeting Parameters

Securing a formal mortgage pre-approval before your HHT begins establishes precise financial boundaries. Knowing your maximum pre-approval figure and estimated monthly overhead in advance allows you to view homes with complete confidence during your short HHT window.

2. Target Neighborhood Selection & Commute Times

Consider what location best matches your lifestyle:

  • Petawawa: Minutes from Garrison gates, near local schools, parks, and quick access to base facilities.
  • Pembroke: Established residential neighborhoods, regional hospital access, and broader retail/dining options.
  • Laurentian Valley & Rural Townships: Larger 1+ acre lots, detached outbuildings, private well/septic systems, and quiet country settings.

3. Structuring Your House Hunting Trip (HHT)

With limited days on the ground during an HHT, efficiency is critical. Working with a dedicated local real estate professional allows you to schedule back-to-back showings, organize property itineraries in advance, and coordinate home inspections without losing precious time.

4. Pre-Listing Preparation for Outbound Sellers

If you are posted out of Petawawa, starting minor cosmetic touch-ups, decluttering, and gathering essential paperwork (utility bills, major update receipts, property tax statements) early ensures your home is market-ready the moment your posting confirmation arrives.
