Choosing a Real Estate Agent in Pembroke & Petawawa: Key Questions to Ask Before You Start

By Prakash Solanki, REALTOR® | RE/MAX Pembroke Realty Ltd., Brokerage • November 26, 2026

Whether you are planning to buy a home or sell a property in Pembroke, Petawawa, Laurentian Valley, or nearby Ottawa Valley communities, choosing the right real estate representation is an important early step. A smooth experience starts with clear communication, realistic expectations, and a solid grasp of local neighbourhoods and Ontario real estate rules.

In my experience helping people navigate local real estate decisions across the Ottawa Valley, asking a few straightforward questions upfront makes it much easier to find an agent whose approach fits your timeline and comfort level.

1. How Well Do You Know Pembroke, Petawawa & Surrounding Areas?

Market conditions, property types, and day-to-day considerations can shift from one neighbourhood or township to the next across Renfrew County.

  • Neighbourhood & Township Context: Ask how an agent evaluates differences between in-town streets, rural lots, municipal services, and well or septic setups.
  • Current Market Activity: Look for clear, factual context on how local homes are positioned and priced rather than broad generalizations.
  • Lifestyle Fit: Make sure they take time to understand your commute, space needs, and long-term plans across Pembroke, Petawawa, Laurentian Valley, Deep River, Laurentian Hills, and nearby areas.

2. How Does Client Representation Work Under Ontario Rules?

Real estate transactions in Ontario are governed by the Real Estate Council of Ontario (RECO) and the Trust in Real Estate Services Act (TRESA), which outline clear rules around representation, disclosure, and written agreements.

  • Clear Written Agreements: A knowledgeable agent will walk you through how representation agreements work and what duties are owed before you sign anything.
  • Transparent Communication: Ask how conflicts, multiple offers, or changing plans are handled so you always know where you stand.
  • Accurate Claims: Ensure any marketing or guidance you receive sticks to verified facts and clear terms without misleading promises.

3. What Is Your Communication & Next-Steps Process?

Buying or selling involves coordinating details, timelines, and decisions step by step. Asking how an agent communicates helps set clear expectations from day one:

  • For Buyers: Discuss how you will narrow down neighbourhoods, review property details, arrange showings, and structure offers responsibly.
  • For Sellers: Talk through preparation priorities, pricing analysis, marketing presentation, and how showing feedback is shared.
  • Responsiveness & Updates: Confirm how quickly calls, texts, or emails are returned and how milestones are tracked as closing approaches.
