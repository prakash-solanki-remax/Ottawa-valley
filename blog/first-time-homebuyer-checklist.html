First-Time Buyer Guide

First-Time Homebuyer Checklist

Published by Prakash Solanki, REALTOR® | RE/MAX Pembroke Realty Ltd., Brokerage

Buying your first home is an exciting milestone, especially when you are exploring welcoming communities like Deep River, Laurentian Valley, and Petawawa. Each area offers its own character—from riverfront proximity and wooded lots to established residential neighbourhoods—and having a clear checklist helps you move forward with confidence rather than guesswork.

1. Map Out Your Budget, Down Payment & Closing Costs

Before touring properties, take a clear look at your overall financial picture. In Ontario, first-time buyers often explore savings tools such as the First Home Savings Account (FHSA), the RRSP Home Buyers' Plan, and available first-time land transfer tax rebates to support their purchase plan.

  • Down Payment & Monthly Comfort: Confirm a monthly payment that fits comfortably within your day-to-day lifestyle, keeping property taxes, utilities, and maintenance in mind.
  • Closing Costs Reserve: Set aside funds beyond your down payment for legal fees, title insurance, property tax adjustments, and home inspection costs.
  • Steady Credit & Finances: Once pre-approved, avoid opening new credit accounts or taking on large new debts before closing day.

2. Secure a Mortgage Pre-Approval Early

Getting pre-approved helps clarify your realistic price range before you start shopping seriously. A lender or mortgage broker can walk you through stress-test qualification rules, interest rate holds, and amortization options so you know where you stand when the right home appears.

3. Compare Neighbourhoods & Property Types

Because Deep River, Laurentian Valley, and Petawawa offer varied housing stock, think about how each community fits your daily routine:

  • Petawawa: Popular for convenient access to local amenities, schools, parks, and active residential neighbourhoods.
  • Laurentian Valley: Offers a mix of convenient proximity to Pembroke alongside larger lots, country roads, and rural settings.
  • Deep River: Known for its scenic river setting, established tree-lined streets, and tight-knit community feel.

4. Prioritise a Thorough Home Inspection

Whether you are looking at an established home in Deep River or a newer build in Petawawa or Laurentian Valley, a professional home inspection is essential. Paying close attention to the roof, foundation, windows, insulation, and heating system helps you understand both immediate priorities and long-term upkeep. For properties outside municipal services, you will also want to verify details such as well water, septic systems, and heating fuel sources.
