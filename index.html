Local Heritage & Roots

The Spirit & History of the Ottawa Valley

More than just a place to live, the Ottawa Valley is a region steeped in pioneering history, legendary river culture, and deep community pride.

From Timber Pioneers to Modern Communities

A Legacy Carved Along the Ottawa River

Long before becoming a haven for families, military personnel, and outdoor enthusiasts, the Ottawa Valley was the beating heart of Canada’s booming 19th-century timber industry. Hardy lumberjacks navigated the mighty Ottawa River, driving massive square timber rafts downstream toward Montreal and Quebec City.

Towns like Pembroke grew into bustling commercial hubs fueled by the trade, while Petawawa evolved from rugged wilderness into one of Canada's most vital military communities, and Deep River rose as a beacon of scientific innovation during the mid-20th century.
